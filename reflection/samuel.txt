1. What was your primary contribution to the project?

2. What was the most challenging technical problem your team encountered?
-> The most challenging part of the project was trying to figure out why the warning light was not turning off when the program ended.

3. How did your team use testing or debugging to solve a problem?
-> We used testing to check if our temperature range was being implemented properly. We used temperatures that were off the acceptable range by        1 degree to see if we were going to get a warning.

4. Which skills from CSE 1106 were most useful?
-> The most important skills from CSE 1106 were testing, circuit wiring, and raspberry Pi.

5. What would you improve if you had more time?
